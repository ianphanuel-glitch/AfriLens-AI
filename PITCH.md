# AfriLens AI - Pitch Deck

## 🎯 The Problem

### African SMEs are drowning in paper receipts

**The Reality:**
- Small businesses process 50-200 receipts per month
- Manual data entry takes 5-10 minutes per receipt
- **That's 4-33 hours per month** wasted on transcription
- Human errors cost money and time
- Receipts sit in folders, unused for insights

**The Pain:**
- No time for strategic decisions
- No visibility into spending patterns
- Can't track vendor relationships
- Accounting is a nightmare
- Growth is limited by administrative burden

---

## ✨ The Solution

### AfriLens AI: Vision Intelligence for African SMEs

**What it does:**
> Take a photo. AfriLens thinks. You get clean numbers.

**How it works:**
1. **Upload** a receipt photo (phone, camera, scanner)
2. **AI extracts** structured data in seconds
3. **Trust Score** shows confidence level (0-100)
4. **Flags** highlight what needs review
5. **Insights** reveal spending patterns

**The Result:**
- ⏱️ **99% time savings**: 5 minutes → 5 seconds
- ✅ **Higher accuracy**: AI + validation beats manual entry
- 📊 **Actionable insights**: See where money goes
- 🔄 **Seamless integration**: Export to Excel/CSV
