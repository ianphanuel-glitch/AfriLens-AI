# AfriLens AI - Demo Script

**Duration**: 120 seconds (2 minutes)  
**Target**: Non-technical judges  
**Goal**: Show complete value proposition in one perfect flow

---

## 🎬 Demo Flow

### [0:00 - 0:15] Opening Hook

**Say:**
> "African SMEs waste hours manually entering receipt data. What if AI could do it in seconds? Meet AfriLens AI."

**Show:**
- Dashboard with clean, professional UI
- Point to empty state: "No receipts yet"

**Key Point:** Professional, investable look

---

### [0:15 - 0:45] Core Demo - Upload & Process

**Say:**
> "Let me show you how it works. I'll upload a real receipt from a local vendor."

**Actions:**
1. Click upload area (or drag & drop)
2. Select a sample receipt image
3. **Show "AI Thinking..." state** - "This is where the magic happens"
4. Wait for results (3-5 seconds)

**Say:**
> "In just seconds, AfriLens extracted:
> - Vendor name: Nakumatt Supermarket
> - Date: January 15th, 2024
> - Total: 3,200 Kenyan Shillings
> - And it even found individual line items"

**Show:**
- Results card with extracted data
- **Point to Trust Score badge** - "87% - This is our confidence level"
- **Point to flags** - "It flagged a minor total mismatch, so you know to verify"

**Key Point:** Fast, accurate, transparent

---

### [0:45 - 1:00] Trust Score Explanation

**Say:**
> "Every receipt gets a Trust Score from 0 to 100. This tells you how confident we are in the extraction. 87% means it's highly reliable, but we flagged one issue for you to review."

**Show:**
- Trust score badge (color-coded)
- Flags section
- Explain: "Green = high confidence, Yellow = medium, Red = needs review"

**Key Point:** Transparency builds trust

---

### [1:00 - 1:30] Insights & Value

**Say:**
> "But AfriLens doesn't just extract data - it provides insights."

**Show:**
- Insight banner: "You've spent 3,200 KES at Nakumatt across 2 receipts"
- Stats cards:
  - Total spent: KES 15,400
  - Average trust score: 85%
  - Today's receipts: 3

**Say:**
> "Now you can see:
> - Where you're spending money
> - Your top vendors
> - Daily transaction patterns
> - All in one dashboard"

**Key Point:** Business intelligence, not just data extraction

---

### [1:30 - 1:50] Export & Integration

**Say:**
> "And you can export everything to CSV or Excel for your accounting software."

**Actions:**
1. Click "Export Excel"
2. Show download (or mention it downloads)

**Say:**
> "This integrates with your existing workflow. No manual entry needed."

**Key Point:** Practical, not just a demo

---

### [1:50 - 2:00] Closing - Why This Wins

**Say:**
> "AfriLens AI transforms hours of manual work into seconds of AI processing. It's built specifically for African SMEs - handling local currencies, date formats, and vendor patterns. Every extraction comes with a trust score, so you know what to verify."

**Show:**
- Receipts table with multiple entries
- Point to trust scores
- Point to flags

**Final Line:**
> "Take a photo. AfriLens thinks. You get clean numbers. That's AfriLens AI."

---

## 🎯 Key Messages to Emphasize

1. **Speed**: Seconds, not minutes
2. **Accuracy**: Trust scores show confidence
3. **Transparency**: Flags show what to verify
4. **Intelligence**: Insights, not just data
5. **African-focused**: Built for local context

## ⚠️ Backup Plans

**If upload fails:**
- "Let me show you the results from a previous upload" (show receipts table)

**If processing is slow:**
- "The AI is analyzing the image quality and extracting text" (explain what's happening)

**If extraction is imperfect:**
- "Notice the trust score is 75% - that's why we show flags. You can see exactly what needs review."

## 📋 Pre-Demo Checklist

- [ ] Backend running on port 8000
- [ ] Frontend running on port 5173
- [ ] At least 2-3 sample receipts ready
- [ ] Database initialized
- [ ] Tesseract OCR installed and working
- [ ] Browser tab ready with dashboard open

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Practice the timing** - 120 seconds is tight
2. **Have receipts ready** - Don't search during demo
3. **Emphasize Trust Score** - Judges love transparency
4. **Show flags** - Demonstrates intelligence
5. **End strong** - Close with value proposition

**Remember:** You're not just showing a tool - you're showing a solution to a real problem for African SMEs.
